How it works

  1. A senior shares their story. Pasted the way they'd tell a junior on WhatsApp. Hinglish is fine.
  2. Gemma pulls out the facts. Rounds, topics, resources, mistakes and what they wish they knew, each with the exact sentence it came from. If a quote isn't really in the text, the fact is dropped.
  3. The senior checks it. Nothing is saved until they approve it, and they choose whether juniors may contact them.
  4. You get a playbook. What people actually faced, counted across stories, plus who to ask at each stage and a day-by-day plan.
