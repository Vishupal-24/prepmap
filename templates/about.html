About PrepMap

Every time an internship opens, juniors ask seniors the same questions: how was the OA, what did they ask, what should I study. The answers are valuable, but they live in WhatsApp chats and get lost. PrepMap keeps them, counts them and points you to the right person.

How the AI is used

An open-weight model, Gemma, reads each story and pulls out facts: rounds, topics, resources, mistakes and advice. For every fact it must copy a short quote from the story. Plain code then checks that the quote really appears in the text. If it doesn't, the fact is dropped. The model reads; normal code counts, ranks and decides.

Privacy

  • No accounts. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to track you with.
  • Names only with a yes. A name or contact is stored only if the person ticks "juniors may contact me". Everyone else counts anonymously.
  • The original text stays hidden. Story pages show extracted facts, never the full text someone pasted.
  • Delete any time. Every saved story gets a delete link.
  • Runs on your laptop. For real stories, PrepMap runs with local Gemma through Ollama, so nothing leaves the machine. The hosted demo uses made-up sample stories only.
  • Checklists stay in your browser. Your prep plan ticks are saved only on your device.

What it isn't

It doesn't predict whether you'll get selected, and it doesn't rank people. It shows what people reported, how many said it, and how sure we can be.

Open source

The code is on GitHub under the MIT license. Built for the DEV Hacktoberfest 2026 Weekend Challenge: Build for a Friend.
